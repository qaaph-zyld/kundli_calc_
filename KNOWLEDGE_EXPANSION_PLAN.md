## Quality Standards

### Every Interpretation Must Include:
- ✅ Chapter and verse references
- ✅ Original text name and author
- ✅ Translator name and publication
- ✅ Literal translation
- ✅ Detailed effects list
- ✅ Positive and challenging effects separated
- ✅ Remedies (when provided in source)
- ✅ Timing information
- ✅ Confidence level (based on source clarity)

### Never Include:
- ❌ AI-generated interpretations
- ❌ Modern astrological additions without classical basis
- ❌ Unattributed "common knowledge"
- ❌ Personal opinions or modern reinterpretations
